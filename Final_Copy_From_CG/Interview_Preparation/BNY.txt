Functional Title:  QA Automation

    Conduct QA reviews of sanctions operations L1 and L2 alert dispositions for static and transactional sanctions hits 
    Document and report any defects or issues discovered during the review process, providing clear descriptions and relevant details and support for each defect or issue.
    Ensure compliance with established policies, procedures, and regulatory requirements.

To be successful in this role, we’re seeking the following: 

    Bachelor’s degree or equivalent combination of education and work experience required
    At least 3 years of financial crime-related alert review experience (with a preference for sanctions alerts) or testing/QA experience 
    Strong understanding of fundamental test/QA processes
    Strong analytical, problem-solving, organizational and time management skills
    Ability to self-manage workload and goals independently in a fast-paced and deadline-driven organization
